Privacy Notice.

How information submitted through this independent professional consulting website is handled.

LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 16, 2026

This notice applies to this website for Joseph Dayan, MD's independent medical consulting and expert witness activities. It does not apply to the University of Miami, Jackson Health System, or their patient care services.

Information we collect

When the attorney inquiry form is activated and you choose to submit it, we receive the contact and business information you provide, such as your name, organization, professional email, optional phone number, topic of inquiry, timeframe, and general message. Website hosting and security services may also process basic technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request time.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to respond to requests, evaluate whether a potential engagement may be appropriate, perform preliminary administrative screening, and manage professional communications. Submitting an inquiry does not create an expert engagement or a confidential, attorney-client, or physician-patient relationship.

No patient information through this site

Please do not submit medical records, protected health information, patient or party names, privileged communications, or other confidential case materials through the public inquiry form. If an engagement is agreed upon, an appropriate separate process for transferring materials will be arranged.

Service providers

The website may use hosting infrastructure and, once configured, Resend to transmit form notifications by email. Those services may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide hosting or email delivery. The site also requests typography resources from Google Fonts, which may receive technical information about browser requests.

Analytics and cookies

No advertising trackers or marketing analytics are intentionally installed in this version of the website. Essential infrastructure may use technical controls to maintain site security and operation. Any future tracking technologies should be reflected in an updated notice.

Disclosure and retention

Contact information may be shared with service providers assisting with website operation, with professional advisers where appropriate, or when required by law. Inquiry correspondence may be retained as needed for business records, applicable legal obligations, and dispute resolution. No fixed retention period is represented here.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used for handling inquiries, but no public web form or electronic communication can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid submitting sensitive information through this website.

Contact and updates

Questions about website privacy can be raised through the attorney inquiry page without including confidential case information. This notice may be updated as functionality, service providers, or business practices change.

Professional disclaimer

This website provides general information about independent professional consulting. It does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or medical services to patients. University and hospital affiliations are factual professional background and do not imply endorsement of private consulting activities.
