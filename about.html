BACKGROUND & CREDENTIALS

Experience shaped by multiple disciplines.

A career combining pediatric medicine, complex anesthesia, critical care, and medical education.

JD
JOSEPH DAYAN, MD, FAAP

Physician. Educator.
Medical specialist.

Dr. Joseph Dayan is an anesthesiologist and pediatric critical care physician whose clinical career bridges several closely connected fields.

He serves as Assistant Professor of Clinical Anesthesiology at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine, with clinical work in pediatric cardiac anesthesiology and pediatric critical care.

His work includes anesthetic and perioperative care for children and adults with congenital heart disease. He also participates in the education of medical students, residents, and fellows and serves as Director of the Pediatric Anesthesiology Fellowship Program.

His postgraduate training encompasses general pediatrics, pediatric critical care, general anesthesiology, pediatric anesthesiology, and pediatric cardiac anesthesiology.

Professional affiliation

University of Miami Miller School of Medicine and Holtz Children's Hospital / Jackson Memorial Hospital. These affiliations identify Dr. Dayan's clinical and academic roles; expert witness consulting is independent of, and not endorsed by, these institutions.
