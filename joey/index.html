ABOUT DR. DAYAN

Clinician and medical educator.

Dr. Joseph Dayan is an Assistant Professor of Clinical Anesthesiology at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine. His clinical work spans pediatric anesthesiology, pediatric cardiac anesthesiology, and pediatric critical care.

He directs the University of Miami Pediatric Anesthesiology Fellowship Program and completed subspecialty training at institutions including the University of Pennsylvania (Children's Hospital of Philadelphia), Mayo Clinic, and the University of Miami.
