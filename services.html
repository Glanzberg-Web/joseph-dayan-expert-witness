MEDICAL-LEGAL SERVICES

Thoughtful review.
Useful clarity.

Services designed to help legal teams understand complex clinical decisions and evaluate medical questions within Dr. Dayan's areas of expertise.

SCOPE OF SERVICES

The right expertise for the right question.

All services are offered subject to case screening, appropriate scope, scheduling, and a separate engagement agreement.

01

Medical Record Review

Review of relevant medical records, timelines, treatment decisions, and the clinical circumstances surrounding a potential case. The goal is to identify and explain issues that warrant further medical analysis.

02

Standard-of-Care Analysis

Evaluation of care within the pertinent specialty, care environment, and clinical circumstances. Any professional opinion follows careful review of appropriate records and relevant medical context.

03

Case Consultation

Medical insight to help attorneys understand case-specific clinical questions, medical terminology, alternative explanations, and potential areas requiring further investigation.

04

Expert Reports & Testimony

Written opinions, deposition support, and courtroom testimony may be considered when appropriate to the matter, confirmed qualifications, and terms of the individual engagement. Prior testimony experience is not represented by this website.

HOW INQUIRIES WORK

A measured process.

STEP 01

Initial Inquiry

Share an overview of the legal matter without confidential records or protected patient information.

STEP 02

Fit & Conflicts

Discuss the specialty questions, timing, possible conflicts, and whether a review may be appropriate.

STEP 03

Engagement

If mutually appropriate, establish scope, terms, and a secure process for sharing case materials before substantive review.

PROFESSIONAL OBJECTIVITY

Clinical reasoning, not advocacy.

Medical-legal analysis should begin with facts and established medical knowledge rather than a preferred result. Any opinion is based on the particulars of the case and Dr. Dayan's professional assessment.

This website does not promise any particular opinion, report, outcome, acceptance of a case, or availability to testify. Consultation alone does not create an expert engagement or confidential relationship.
